7.1 Account and profile data: for the duration of your account, then up to 30 days after closure to allow restoration.

11.1 The MyAdminMate mobile app does not use cookies.

11.2 Our website hosts links to download the app from the Apple App Store and Google Play. It does not collect personal data through forms, account registration, or similar means. The only non-essential cookies the website uses are analytics cookies, which help us understand how visitors use the site. A strictly necessary cookie may also be used to remember your cookie preferences; this does not require your consent.

11.3 All cookies used on our website are used in accordance with current Cookie Law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, as amended) and UK GDPR. Analytics cookies may process limited information such as your IP address and how you interact with the site, and are only placed with your consent.

11.4 Before any analytics cookies are placed on your device, you will be shown a consent banner requesting your consent. You may refuse them, and you may withdraw or change your consent at any time through the cookie settings on our website. Refusing analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the website. You can also control cookies through your internet browser settings.

11.5 The specific analytics cookies currently in use, including their provider, purpose, and duration, are listed in the cookie settings available on our website.

We do not record audio in the background and do not use voice data for advertising, voiceprint identification, or unrelated purposes. You may revoke microphone access through your device settings.
